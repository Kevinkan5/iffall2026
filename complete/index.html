Kevin's Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

A simple 5-ingredient pasta dish.

Disclaimer: this is not a traditional recipe for Aglio e Olio.

  • Yield 1-2 servings
  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Total 30 min

Ingredients

  • A handful of spaghetti (~150 g)
  • Water, four cups
  • Salt
  • 2/3 cup high quality extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3-4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp crushed red pepper flakes, to taste
  • 1/4 cup flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
  • Pasta water (important for forming the sauce)
  • Parmigiano-Reggiano, ideally hand-grated
  • Lemon juice (optional)

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
  2. Add the spaghetti and cook until just shy of al dente (~9 minutes).
  3. While the pasta cooks, chop the parsley fine and slice the garlic thin.
  4. Warm the olive oil in a wide skillet over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and chili flakes and cook gently, swirling the pan often, until the garlic turns pale gold, 4-5 minutes.
  5. Do not burn the garlic, or even brown it.
  6. Turn down the heat and add a splash of starchy pasta water. Mix with the oil until it forms a nice emulsion.
  7. When the pasta is just shy of al dente, add it to the pan with the sauce, along with the parsley.
  8. Add splashes of pasta water and mix until you get your desired consistency. A squeeze of lemon goes here, if you like.
  9. Plate right away and sprinkle on Parmigiano-Reggiano if you like. Enjoy!
