Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

A simple 5-ingredient pasta dish that I made so often to the point where I memorized it completely. Delicious, surprisingly customizable, and great for any occasion.

disclaimer: this is not a traditional recipe for Aglio e Olio.

Yield: 1-2 Servings

Prep: 10 mins | Cook: 15 mins | Total time: 25 mins

Ingredients

  • spaghetti (or any long pasta you have on hand)
  • salt, for the pasta water
  • 2/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3-4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp crushed red pepper flakes, to taste
  • 1/4 cup flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
  • Pasta water. Important for forming the sauce.
  • Parmigiano-Reggiano, ideally hand-grated
  • Lemon juice (optional, but I really like it)

Instructions

  • Bring a large pan of salted water to a boil. I say pan instead of pot because with less water, you get more starch. Add the spaghetti and cook until just shy of al dente (about two minutes less than the package suggests).
  • Don't throw away the starchy water just yet.
  • While the pasta is cooking, chop up your parsely into fine pieces and slice your garlic into thin slices.
  • Warm the olive oil in a wide skillet over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and chili flakes and cook gently, swirling the pan often, until the garlic turns pale gold, four to five minutes.
  • DON'T burn the garlic.
  • When your garlic is good, turn down your head and add a splash of the starchy pasta water. It will sizzle with the oil and form a nice emulsion.
  • When your pasta is just shy of al dente, add it in with the pan with the garlic, along with your parsley.
  • Add splashes of pasta water and stir well. The more pasta water, the "creamier" your sauce. Add a squeeze of lemon if you like.
  • Plate right away once the consistency is good. sprinkle on Parmigiano Reggiano if you like.
  • ENJOY!
